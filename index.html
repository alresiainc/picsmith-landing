Get PixelForge

Not yet listed on the extension stores — for now, download the source and load it as an unpacked extension. It takes under a minute.

Download pixelforge-extension.zip
  1. Unzip the download anywhere on your computer.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions).
  3. Toggle Developer mode on, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped PixelForge folder.
  5. Pin it to the toolbar and open a new design. 🎉
